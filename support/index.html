Operator Support

Need help with Operator, want to report a problem, or have a suggestion? Email operator@cybernetks.be.

Support messages are reviewed as availability permits. A specific response time is not guaranteed, but providing the details below helps us investigate efficiently.

What to include

Please include:

  • the Operator version and build number;
  • your iPhone or iPad model;
  • your iOS or iPadOS version;
  • what you expected to happen;
  • what happened instead; and
  • the steps that reproduce the problem, if known.

A screenshot or short screen recording can help when the issue is visual. Remove or obscure private information before attaching it.

Do not email passwords, authentication codes, or sensitive project and task content. Share only the information needed to investigate your request.

Finding your Operator version

Open Operator → Settings. The version and build number appear near the top of the screen below the Operator name.

Founding Supporter Pass purchases

Open Operator → Settings, then scroll to Support Cybernetks. The optional Founding Supporter Pass is a one-time purchase that provides permanent Founder recognition and lifetime access to Operator Pro in this app. Every feature available in Operator today stays free. The price shown in the purchase button is supplied and localized by StoreKit.

If a purchase is pending

Some purchases need approval from Apple, for example through Ask to Buy. Operator will show that it is waiting for purchase approval and will recognize the pass when Apple completes the purchase. Do not buy the pass again while it is pending.

Restore Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in Settings → Support Cybernetks when you have already bought the pass, such as after installing Operator on another compatible device. Sign in with the same Apple Account that made the original purchase, then select Restore Purchases.

If Operator says that no Founding Supporter purchase was found, Apple did not report an active Founding Supporter entitlement for that Apple Account. Confirm that you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase and that the purchase was completed, then try Restore Purchases again. If the message continues, contact support.

If the pass is unavailable or a purchase cannot complete

If Operator says the Founding Supporter Pass is unavailable, or it could not complete a purchase or restore purchases, select Retry, Try Again, or Restore Purchases as shown. Check your internet connection and try again later. StoreKit can take time to load product information or complete a transaction.

Founder app icon

After a completed Founding Supporter purchase, you can choose either Default or Founder under Founder App Icon in Settings → Support Cybernetks. Choosing Default returns Operator to its standard icon.

Alternate app icons are not supported on every device or configuration. If the Founder option is unavailable or Operator cannot change the app icon, keep the current icon and try again later; the purchase and its benefits are unaffected.

If iCloud synchronization is not working

Check that:

  1. Each device is signed in to the same Apple Account.
  2. iCloud Drive is enabled for the device and Operator is allowed to use iCloud.
  3. Your iCloud account has available storage.
  4. Each device has a working internet connection.
  5. Operator has been opened on each device and given a little time to synchronize.

If the workspace still differs between devices, contact support with the device models, operating-system versions, Operator versions, and a description of which changes are missing.

General troubleshooting

Try these steps in order:

  1. Update Operator to the latest available version.
  2. Install the latest compatible iOS or iPadOS update.
  3. Close and reopen Operator.
  4. Restart the device.
  5. Try the action again and note any message shown by the app.

If the problem continues, send the details listed above to operator@cybernetks.be.

Before deleting or reinstalling Operator

Do not delete or reinstall Operator as an initial troubleshooting step. Workspace changes that have not synchronized to iCloud may be lost. Contact support first if you are considering either action because of a data or synchronization problem.

Contacting support about a purchase

Email operator@cybernetks.be with the Operator version and build number, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the Apple Account country or region if relevant, and the message shown by Operator. Do not send workspace content, payment-card information, passwords, authentication codes, or purchase receipts containing information you do not need to share.

Privacy questions

The Operator Privacy Policy is published separately alongside this support page. For privacy questions or requests, email operator@cybernetks.be.
